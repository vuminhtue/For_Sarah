# Stage 2: Study and data extraction
## 2A–2D. Citation, study, design, and sample
Cross-sectional analysis of 69 young children who stutter drawn from a larger multi-site Midwestern longitudinal stuttering project.

## 2F. Measures
**Temperament:** Children’s Behavior Questionnaire short form, mother/parent report, 94 items, 1–7 scale plus not-applicable; core dimensions include Effortful Control, Surgency, and Negative Affect.

**Language/communication outcome:** parent- and clinician-rated stuttering severity. Standardized speech/language testing was also used for eligibility (normal range), not as the focal continuous language outcome.

**Other predictors:** Confusion, Hubbub and Order Scale (CHAOS) and Life Events Checklist.

**Comparison type:** continuous temperament predicting continuous stuttering severity.
