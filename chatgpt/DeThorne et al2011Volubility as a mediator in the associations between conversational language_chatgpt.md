# Stage 2: Study and data extraction
## 2A–2D. Citation, study, design, and sample
Single analysis from the **Western Reserve Reading Project (WRRP)**, a longitudinal population-based twin study in metropolitan Ohio. Only the first-born twin from each pair was analyzed (n=161). Language samples came from Home Visits 2 and 3; temperament was measured between those visits.

## 2F. Measures
**Temperament:** Children’s Behavior Questionnaire—Short Form; parent report; 7-point response format; Rothbart Surgency, Effortful Control, Negative Affectivity and facets.

**Language:** 15-minute conversational samples during a modeling-clay activity, yielding total complete/intelligible utterances (TCICU), total words, mean length of utterance (MLU), conjunctions, different words, and Measure D. Some measures were computed both from the entire sample and standardized to 100 C-units to control volubility.

**Comparison type:** continuous temperament versus continuous conversational-language measures; biological sex tested as a possible moderator.

## Overall quality rating
**Fair.** The population, measures, and language-sampling protocol are strong, but the focal exposure is not clearly temporally prior to the outcome, analyses are mainly unadjusted correlations, and participation/follow-up/blinding information is incomplete.
