# Stage 2: Study and data extraction
## 2A–2D. Citation, study, design, and sample
Two independent cross-sectional U.S. online samples recruited through MTurk: 460 children aged 16–30 months and 328 children aged 31–42 months. The article reports 369 additional recruited cases excluded primarily for failed validity checks and a small number for genetic disorders. Data collection spanned approximately Dec 2015–Apr 2019.

## 2F. Measures
**Temperament:** ECBQ short form, parent report, 1=Never to 7=Always. Dimensions: Negative Affect, Surgency, Effortful Control, with multiple facets. Dimension alpha ranges were about .67–.85 in the younger group and .70–.84 in the older group; most facet alphas were .57–.83/.65–.83, with low Impulsivity alpha (.31/.34). Rothbart-based: **Yes**.

**Language (16–30 mo):** MCDI Words & Sentences, 680-word expressive vocabulary checklist; total vocabulary and noun count.

**Language (31–42 mo):** MCDI-III, 100-word vocabulary plus grammar and language-complexity items.

**Additional language:** retrospective age of first word combinations, number of new words in past 2 weeks, and MLU from longest-sentence examples; MLU inter-rater reliability >90%.

**Comparison type:** continuous temperament versus continuous language outcomes, using bivariate correlations, partial correlations controlling parent personality, and regression/hierarchical regression.

## Overall quality rating
**Fair.** Large samples, explicit power, validated measures, and adjustment analyses are strengths. The cross-sectional, common-parent-reporter design limits temporal inference and creates potential shared-method bias.
