# Stage 2: Study and data extraction
## 2A–2D. Citation, study, design, and sample
Method-validation study using archived lab videos collected 2015–2020. Total n=516 (255 female), with 22 missing caregiver temperament and 3 missing vocabulary information for relevant analyses. Videos came from three U.S. geographic locations.

## 2F. Measures
**Caregiver temperament:** very-short ECBQ, 7-point scale, dimensions Negative Affect, Surgency, Effortful Control.

**Thin-slice temperament:** 18-item ECBQ-derived observer form (6 items/domain), 7-point scale, scored by naïve raters from three video slices (first 3 min, middle ~5 min, final ~5 min). Reliability and convergent/divergent validity were explicitly evaluated.

**Language:** MacArthur-Bates CDI Words & Sentences (≤30 months; 680 words) and MCDI-III (>30 months; 100 words); caregiver-reported expressive vocabulary count. Vocabulary percentile was also analyzed across the full sample.

**Comparison type:** continuous temperament versus continuous vocabulary as criterion-validity evidence.
