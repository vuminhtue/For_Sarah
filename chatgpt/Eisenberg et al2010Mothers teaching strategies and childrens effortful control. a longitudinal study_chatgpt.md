# Stage 2: Study and data extraction
## 2A–2D. Citation, study, design, and sample
Longitudinal study with assessments at approximately 18, 30, and 42 months. Ns were 255, 229, and 209 respectively (after one family with father rather than mother report was omitted from the focal mother-teaching analysis).

## 2F. Measures
**Temperament/EC:** mother and nonparent-caregiver reports using ECBQ dimensions at 18/30 months and CBQ at 42 months, plus observed effortful-control indicators. Report scales were 1–7. Reported alphas for core attention/inhibitory subscales were generally acceptable (roughly .69–.90 across reporter/wave). Rothbart-based: **Yes**.

**Language:** MacArthur-Bates Communicative Development Inventory short forms; Level II/age-appropriate vocabulary checklists at younger waves and an older-child form at 42 months. Score = number of words reportedly spoken (English or Spanish).

**Comparison type:** repeated continuous EC versus continuous vocabulary, alongside the paper’s primary longitudinal mother-teaching models.
