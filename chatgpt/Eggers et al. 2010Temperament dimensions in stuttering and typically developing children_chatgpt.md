# Stage 2: Study and data extraction
## 2A–2D. Citation, study, design, and sample
Cross-sectional matched-group study of 58 children who stutter (CWS) and 58 typically developing children (TDC), recruited in the Dutch-speaking part of Belgium.

## 2F. Measures
**Temperament:** Dutch Children’s Behavior Questionnaire (CBQ-D), caregiver/mother report, 233 items and 18 scales in the Dutch adaptation. The Rothbart higher-order dimensions are Extraversion/Surgency, Negative Affectivity, and Effortful Control. Average internal consistency for the study scales is reported around alpha=.74.

**Language/communication outcome:** developmental stuttering group status and clinician-rated stuttering severity.

**Comparison type:** language/communication-disorder group versus typically developing group on temperament; within CWS, severity correlated with temperament.
