# Stage 2: Study and data extraction

## 2A–2D. Citation, study, design, and sample
Single cross-sectional analysis nested in a larger longitudinal developmental-outcomes study. Testing occurred in families’ homes. Final groups were TH n=59 and DHH n=58; one DHH language score was missing, yielding n=57 for language analyses.

## 2F. Measures

**Temperament:** caregiver CBQ short form, 94 items, 1–7 response scale; effortful control, negative affectivity, and surgency–extraversion. Subscale internal consistency ranged approximately .62–.88, with most scales ≥.70. Rothbart-based: **Yes**.

**Language:** CELF Preschool–2 Concepts & Following Directions for younger children or CELF–5 Following Directions for older children; norm-referenced scaled scores indexing spoken-language comprehension.

**Comparison type:** continuous temperament scores related to continuous language scores, with family-related environmental confusion (CHAOS) tested as a moderator; TH and DHH groups analyzed separately.
