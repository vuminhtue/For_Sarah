# 3. Measures

## 3.1 Temperament
The study used a multimethod approach to assess **Effortful Control (EC)**:
- **Parent/Caregiver Reports:**
    - **ECBQ (T1, T2):** Three 12-item scales (attention-focusing, attention shifting, inhibitory control). 1–7 scale. $\alpha = .69$ to $.88$.
    - **CBQ (T3):** Subscales for attention focusing (14 items), attention shifting (12 items), and inhibitory control (13 items). 1–7 scale. $\alpha = .67$ to $.82$.
- **Observational/Behavioral:**
    - **Snack Delay Task (T1, T2, T3):** Children rated (1–4) for self-restraint while waiting for candy.
    - **Infant Behavior Record (IBR) (T1, T2, T3):** Rated by four researchers on attention and persistence (1–5 scale). $\alpha = .73$ to $.84$.
- **Rothbart-based:** Yes (ECBQ and CBQ).

## 3.2 Language
- **Construct:** General Vocabulary (Expressive).
- **Instrument:** MacArthur Communicative Development Inventories (CDI) Short Form Level II (T1, T2) and Level IV (T3).
- **Measure:** Total number of words spoken (in English or Spanish).
- **Assessment Time:** T1, T2, T3.

## 3.3 Other relevant measures
- **Maternal Teaching Strategies:** Observed during a 3-minute task; coded for Directives, Questioning, and Cognitive Assistance (1–3 scale).
- **SES:** Standardized mean of parental education and family income.

### Substantive Conclusion
There are consistent, significant positive correlations between child vocabulary and multiple indices of effortful control (particularly mother-reported EC and behavioral measures like the snack delay task). In the SEM models, vocabulary was used as a covariate for EC at each time point, indicating a stable association between language and temperament.

---

# 6. Additional Associations Relevant to Interpretation

- **SES and EC:** SES was significantly positively associated with T1 EC in all three SEM models (p. 10).
- **Age/Sex and EC:** Girls and older children were higher in EC (unstandardized coefficients .13–.19, $p < .02$) (p. 10).
- **EC and Teaching Strategies:** T1 EC positively predicted T2 maternal cognitive assistance and questioning, and negatively predicted T2 maternal directives (p. 10).

## Overall NIH quality judgment

**Overall rating: Good**

### Strengths
- Multimethod and multireporter approach for temperament (EC).
- Strong longitudinal design (three waves) using structural equation modeling.
- Appropriate control for key confounders (SES, age, sex, and vocabulary).
- Low attrition rate (<20%).

### Main risks of bias and limitations
- Lack of explicit reporting on the monolingual status of the sample.
- Absence of power analysis or sample size justification.
- No blinding reported for observational coders (though coding occurred months after data collection).

---

# 8. Final Review Decision

**Include.** The study provides high-quality longitudinal evidence of the association between a core Rothbart temperament dimension (Effortful Control) and expressive language ability (vocabulary).
