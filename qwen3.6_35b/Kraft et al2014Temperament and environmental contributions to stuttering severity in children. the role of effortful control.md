# 3. Measures

## 3.1 Temperament
- **Construct/Facet:** Effortful Control (EC), Surgency/Extraversion, Negative Affectivity
- **Instrument:** Children's Behavior Questionnaire-Short Form (CBQ-SF)
- **Informant:** Mothers
- **Items/Scales:** 94 items total; EC, Surgency, Negative Affectivity aggregated as primary domains
- **Response Scale:** 1 (extremely untrue) to 7 (extremely true)
- **Reliability:** Cronbach’s α ≥ 0.70 for 11/12 scales; acceptable for sadness (0.65)
- **Time Point:** Single initial visit
- **Rothbart-based:** Yes

## 3.2 Language
- **Construct:** Stuttering severity (frequency, duration, tension, secondary behaviors)
- **Instrument/Task:** 0–7 severity scale (Table 1); spontaneous speech samples (~30 min each on 2 days)
- **Category:** Speech production/fluency disorder severity (qualifies as verbal expression/communication outcome per protocol)
- **Score Type:** Categorical severity ratings averaged/rated globally (mother) or from structured sample (clinician)
- **Reliability/Validity:** Inter/intra-rater reliability >97% for clinician ratings; mother severity validated against clinician ratings (r=0.46, p<0.01)
- **Time Point:** Single visit (mother global average; clinician lab sample)

## 3.3 Other relevant measures
- **Home Environment:** Confusion, Hubbub and Order Scale (CHAOS); 15 items, 5-point scale; α=0.79, test-retest r=0.74
- **Life Events:** Life Events Checklist (LEC); parent-reported yes/no for events in past 12 months
- **Location:** Methods, pp. 83–84

The association is consistent across informant sources but remains correlational.

### Overall NIH quality judgment

**Overall rating: Fair**

#### Strengths
- Clear objectives and well-defined population with strict eligibility criteria.
- Robust reliability testing for both temperament and severity measures.
- Appropriate statistical modeling (HLM) for the research design.

#### Main risks of bias and limitations
- Cross-sectional design prevents temporal or causal inference.
- No power analysis; small sample size limits generalizability and statistical power.
- Key confounders (SES, race/ethnicity, home stress beyond CHAOS) not measured or controlled.
- Lack of blinding and single-time measurement reduce internal validity.

---

# 8. Final Review Decision

**Protocol decision: Include** (with manual review flagged for 1D monolingual/typical development criteria).
**Scientific relevance notes:** The study directly addresses the link between a core Rothbart temperament dimension (Effortful Control) and a qualifying communication outcome (stuttering severity). Findings support the hypothesis that self-regulatory temperament traits influence speech disorder severity, offering clinical implications for targeted intervention. Manual verification of monolingual status and exact age SD is recommended for replication precision.
