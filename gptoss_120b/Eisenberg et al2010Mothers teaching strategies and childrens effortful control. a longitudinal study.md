### Summary of Findings  

- **Positive associations** were consistently observed between higher effortful control (especially mother‑report) and larger expressive vocabularies across all three waves.  
- **Cross‑lagged SEM** indicated that vocabulary at T2 predicted EC at T2 (significant) and a trend at T3, suggesting reciprocal influence.  
- **No significant links** were reported between the three maternal teaching‑strategy dimensions (directives, questioning, cognitive assistance) and vocabulary (text: “no significant correlations between maternal strategies and children’s vocabulary” p. 8).

## Overall NIH quality judgment  

**Overall rating: Fair**  

The study clearly defines its population, uses validated Rothbart‑based temperament measures, and employs a robust longitudinal design with appropriate covariate adjustment. However, the absence of a reported power analysis, unclear participation rate, and lack of information on blinding of outcome assessors limit confidence, yielding a fair overall quality rating.

---  

# 8. Final Review Decision  

**Protocol decision:** **Include**. The article meets the core inclusion criteria (child age, Rothbart‑based temperament, qualifying language measure, empirical English report, and reports direct temperament‑language associations). Despite some uncertainty regarding monolingual status, the study provides valuable longitudinal data on effortful control and expressive vocabulary, making it relevant for synthesis.
