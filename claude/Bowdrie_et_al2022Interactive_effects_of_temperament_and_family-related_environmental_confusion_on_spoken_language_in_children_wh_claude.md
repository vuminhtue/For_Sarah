## Overall Screening Recommendation

**Manual review required**

The study clearly meets criteria 1A, 1B, 1C, 1E, 1F, 1G, and 1H with strong evidence. However, criterion 1D is ambiguous: the DHH subgroup (half the combined sample) has sensorineural hearing loss, a sensory/developmental condition that is not simply "language delay or language disorder" as the exclusion criteria define it, though the study explicitly excluded children with other neurodevelopmental difficulties and required normal nonverbal IQ. Because the article reports both a typically-hearing subgroup and a DHH subgroup analyzed separately, a reviewer should decide whether to include only the TH subgroup data, both subgroups, or exclude entirely, per the instruction to describe subgroups separately rather than assign automatic exclusion.

## 2A. Citation Verification

## 2B. Relevant Study Number

- Single study, two participant groups (TH and DHH), analyzed separately. Both groups are relevant because the study directly tests the temperament–language relationship (moderated by environmental confusion) in each group.

## 2C. Study Design and Data Source

- **Design:** Cross-sectional, correlational/moderation design
- **Assessment waves:** One assessment point (single home visit); families were part of a larger longitudinal study on developmental outcomes in children who are DHH, but this paper reports a single cross-sectional wave.
- **Larger study:** Yes — families sequentially recruited from a larger longitudinal study on developmental outcomes in children who are DHH.
- **Recruitment setting:** Families from Ohio and Indiana; testing conducted in families' homes during a 1.5–2.5 hr visit.

## 2D. Sample Size

- **Total enrolled:** 59 TH + 58 DHH = 117
- **Final analytic sample:** 59 TH (df=56 for partial correlations); 58 DHH (df=54 for partial correlations); one DHH child (HA user) did not complete the CELF Preschool-2, reducing DHH language N to 57 for that measure.
- **DHH subgroups:** 26 HA users, 32 CI users (30 bilateral CI, 2 bimodal HA+CI)
- **Reasons for exclusions:** One DHH child unable to complete CELF Preschool-2 (n=57 for that measure); audiometric PTA data available only for subsets (aided PTA n=35 DHH; unaided PTA n=24 DHH) due to repeated unsuccessful attempts to obtain data from audiologists.

## Overall Quality Rating

**Fair**

The study has a clearly stated objective, well-defined population with explicit inclusion criteria, validated measures, and appropriate statistical control for a key confound (caregiver education). However, it is a single-timepoint cross-sectional design (precluding several longitudinal-design NIH items), lacks power/sample-size justification, and provides no blinding of assessors. The DHH sample's audiometric data were incomplete for many participants. These limitations, balanced against the clear design and appropriate covariate control, support a "Fair" rather than "Good" or "Poor" rating.
