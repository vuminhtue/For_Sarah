## Overall Screening Recommendation

**Exclude**

The study measures both temperament (CBQ) and language (PPVT, EVT, TELD, MLU, PCC) in the same children, but it does not report any direct statistical association between a temperament variable and a language variable. Group comparisons on temperament and on language are conducted independently. Criterion 1H is not met. Additionally, monolingual status (1D) is not reported, and the primary population consists of children who stutter rather than typically developing children, though there is a typically developing control group.

## 2B. Relevant Study Number

- Single study. The study is relevant in that it collects both temperament and language data from the same longitudinal cohort of children, but it does not test their direct association.

## 2C. Study Design and Data Source

- **Design:** Longitudinal observational cohort study
- **Assessment waves:** 7 visits planned; data reported primarily from Visits 1, 3, and 5 (entry, 1 year later, 2 years later), plus Visit 6 for disfluency data
- **Part of a larger study:** Yes, part of a multi-center longitudinal study of stuttering funded by NIH-NIDCD (R01 DC05210). Related to the broader Illinois longitudinal studies (Yairi & Ambrose, 2005).
- **Recruitment setting:** Four Midwestern university sites — University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, University of Iowa, Northern Illinois University, University of Wisconsin at Milwaukee. Recruited through daycare centers, clinicians, advertisements, self-referrals.

## 2D. Sample Size

- **Total enrolled:** 81 CWS + 40 NFC (controls) = 121
- **Final analytic sample:** 58 classified CWS (19 persistent, 39 recovered) + 40 controls = 98
- **Exclusions:** 23 CWS could not be classified as persistent or recovered because they left the study before determination could be made.
- **Motor subsample (kinematics):** Much smaller — e.g., Visit 5: Persistent = 7, Recovered = 14, Control = 10
- **CBQ sample:** Approximately 91 at Visit 1 (n sizes: Persistent = 19, Recovered = 35, Control = 37)

## Overall Quality Rating

**Fair**

The study has clear objectives, a well-defined population with validated measures, and a longitudinal multi-site design — all strengths. However, there is no power analysis, no blinding, attrition exceeds 20%, and no statistical adjustment for potential confounders. The study does not test the direct temperament–language association relevant to this review. The quality is above "Poor" given its clear design and validated instruments, but the methodological limitations prevent a "Good" rating.
